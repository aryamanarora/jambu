CDIAL

Ralph Lilley Turner. A comparative dictionary of the Indo-Aryan languages. Oxford University Press, 1962–1966. URL: https://dsal.uchicago.edu/dictionaries/soas/.

Notation. Historical citations with heterogeneous original conventions. A macron plus acute identifies a syllable; it is not automatically a modern first-mora accent. Broad dictionary headword and nearest formation are distinct. Grave/svarita and competing old accents remain meaningful.

Access and review. Complete cached original entries read for diagnostic and disputed etymologies; first-pass review of every frozen family. This is not a page-by-page rereading of the entire dictionary.

800 raw source citations · Palula, Sauji

schmidt

Ruth Laila Schmidt and Vijay Kumar Kaul. A comparative analysis of shina and kashmiri vocabularies. Acta Orientalia, 69:231–302, 2008.

Notation. Non-Brokskat first/second doubled-vowel acute represents high-falling/low-rising mora accent. Brokskat notation records syllable stress, not that mora contrast. Short monosyllables may be unmarked.

Access and review. Primary notation discussion pp234, 244–245 and all 269 Brokskat Table 2 items read against print; selected other diagnostic Shina table entries checked. Source glyph quality remains uncertain for some central vowels.
