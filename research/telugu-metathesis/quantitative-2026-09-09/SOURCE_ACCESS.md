# Critical bibliography and source-access record

Investigation dated 9 September 2026. Page numbers below are printed pages unless
marked otherwise. Access to a complete book does not mean that every page was read.
The lexical source keys in the dataset resolve through [source-index.md](source-index.md)
and the frozen [references.tsv](references.tsv). Those metadata are distinct from
independent readings of the sources. Earlier work in the parent directory was checked
and extended; its older access statements do not supersede this record.

Unavailable originals particularly relevant to unresolved cases: Krishnamurti1955
and1978; Emeneau1971; full Burrow–Bhattacharya1963; Schulze1913's exact kakpinai
passage; Israel1979's complete lexicon; Burrow1976 Manda paper pp.40–41; original
photographs for several epigraphic readings. The annotated dataset specifies which
claims rely on later quotation, DEDR attribution or source-labeled secondary evidence.
